## Decisive new find: the Leiden / Louvre Abnormal Hieratic Global Portal (AHGP)

The [official Leiden University Libraries portal](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/papyri-with-abnormal-hieratic-script/) presents **five viewer side records from just THREE physical Louvre papyri**: **E 7851 recto, E 7851 verso, E 7852, E 7856 recto, E 7856 verso**. This grouping is mandatory for all source-level train/validation/test partitions. Portal words/names are visually annotated in the manuscript viewer, and word lookup can surface **hieroglyphic transcription, Egyptological transliteration, morphological type, translation and annotator references**. Its [E 7852 page](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/papyri/p-louvre-e-7852/) publicly presents *numbered* transliteration and translation lines, edition reference K. Donker van Heel (1997), a provenance link and philological notes retaining editorial disagreements. The [project's search documentation](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/search-within-papyri/) specifies per-word indexing and text/annotator fields.

**Why this matters:** Unlike many museum catalogue descriptions, the portal already demonstrates **real authored source-linked reading and interactive visual annotations**. If the asset rights and exact annotation geometry can be contractually cleared, it may remove a substantial amount of manual first-pair annotation labor. The website is **not** evidence that exact line polygons/export bytes or model-usable machine-readable gold are currently downloadable.

**Nonnegotiable dual licence:**
- [Official AHGP content and code licences](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/content-and-code-licenses/) say that **papyrus photographs are copyrighted by the Musée du Louvre** and were supplied to Leiden for this portal as a courtesy. **This is not blanket permission to cache, train on, redistribute, or feed the image to models.** Only Louvre or authorized custodian can grant required photo reuse.
- The **transcriptions, transliterations, translations and other Leiden-owned annotations** are offered under **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 Netherlands**. That grants specified **noncommercial** sharing/adaptation subject to attribution/share-alike terms, **not a permissive commercial-compatible dataset**, and raises separate questions about model weights/ML-derived outputs.
- Software GPL / viewer Apache licences are irrelevant to both original photograph and scholarly content rights.
- Thus, **all three E 7851/E 7852/E 7856 papers remain blocked** for the project's unrestricted corpus. A possible legally isolated noncommercial research side-track still needs Louvre photo permission and legal review of editorial reuse and model/checkpoint obligations. Do not flip DATA-008 production gate.

**Scientific domain boundary:** the [portal's own definition](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/) restricts *Abnormal Hieratic* to the southern administrative script flourishing in Dynasties **25–26** (with origins earlier), whereas the core pilot candidates Cat.1896 and Ramesside Met ostraca are **New Kingdom Hieratic**. AHGP cannot be treated as interchangeable evidence of Ramesside or broader unseen Hieratic proficiency; report a distinct abnormal-Hieratic stratum. Open published 1997 editions imply elevated possible model memorization; only genuine image-conditioned controls and properly unseen sources can later test visual competence.

**Official project provenance and technical clues:** the [Leiden/Louvre colophon](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/colophon/) identifies Musée du Louvre Department of Egyptian Antiquities as the photo collaborator, Leiden Library Centre for Digital Scholarship as technical party, and Koenraad Donker van Heel among source editors. The [platform description](https://lab.library.universiteitleiden.nl/abnormalhieratic/how-to-contribute/) says IIIF Presentation/Image APIs are used and FromThePage can support transcribing/annotating; *does not establish an unrestricted public coordinate/annotation export endpoint or rights to use it*. Seek **the actually authorized original exposure, manifest ID, licensed annotation representation, line/word geometric polygon, text version, correct physical support and reviewer consent**. No guessed URLs or repeated crawling.

## Second finding: direct benchmark-contamination identities outside the original 15

We searched the **full pinned 266 public R-017 source identity records** (metadata only, no gold or pixels) for `7851`, `7852`, `7856`, `Abbott`, `10221`, `Hearst`, `09.184.703`, `09.184.751`, `1896`.

- **Papyrus Abbott / BM EA10221:** direct public benchmark witness **`wm-0021`**. University of Tsukuba's separate [restricted Hieratic Database](https://wdb.jinsha.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/wdb/hdb/about) says Papyrus Abbott was digitized with British Museum cooperation, with photographs © British Museum, and authorised scholarly access only. The manuscript is **directly contaminated at source-identity level**, not a safe hidden training source.
- **Hearst Medical Papyrus:** R-017 public **`wm-0028`** and AKU sign items **`aku-0032`, `aku-0039`, `aku-0112`, `aku-0139`, `aku-0144`** identify the Hearst papyrus support. The [1905 Reisner/Lythgoe facsimile/vocabulary publication](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/0131c4js) is historically useful but cannot be assigned unseen test or training without a full original support/facsimile policy decision; literal same-support benchmark identity is demonstrably present. Do not infer a 1905 publication's original plate imaging rights or exact line alignment from age or open web presence alone.
- **Louvre E 7851, E 7852, E 7856:** **zero exact accession-string matches** in R-017 metadata (at this pinned revision). That is **not scientific novelty or zero overlap**: alternate captions, cropped facsimiles, publication editions and upstream model pretraining remain unverified.
- **Turin Cat.1896 and Met 561345/561392:** zero direct accession-string hits still requires alias/fragment/perceptual/textual comparison and actual image identity.

**Important:** the benchmark's 266 rows are original-source metadata evidence, not a manifest of every image or publication a model may have encountered. The two sealed items remain entirely uninspected.
