## Fixed algorithms and acceptance metrics (frozen now)

- (A) **Form-only baseline:** choose most frequent publisher `pos` among exact matching Egyptian written forms in training, voting one count per independent **source-text ID**, tie by lexicographic POS. Unknown forms explicitly abstain. Do not guess a tag from English AED or German test references.
- (B) **Evidence-gated local context:** choose among already-attested POS tags for the *same* Egyptian form using immediate Egyptian preceding and following original written forms (two-sided first, left then right), but ONLY when a competing tag has **at least two independent training source-text IDs** in that context. Otherwise use the form-only majority. Never emit a POS tag without any training evidence for the exact Egyptian form. Keep alternatives, full distinct-support counts and provenance.
- (C) **Morphological candidate lattice** of exact Egyptian form and POS from original train-only publisher tags: `genus`, `numerus`, `status`, `inflection`, `voice`, `verbalClass`; each field candidate is reported with distinct training text IDs and unknown is explicit. Do NOT assert inflection from spelling or infer semantic roles from POS.
- **Structural feature**, not output translation: for each adjacent pair of observed POS predictions, record original-token order, explicit POS ambiguity, and corpus-level distinct training-source support for POS bigrams. This is *only an adjacency diagnostic*, NOT a syntactic dependency parse. Never use German sentence reference or source targets to recover POS.
- Primary outcome: POS **micro accuracy among tokens with publisher POS** over ALL such tokens (abstentions counted **wrong**), and coverage and conditional correctness separately; also compare baseline vs context, POS candidate-set recall, per-source-group macro correctness, unknown/ambiguous and context-backoff counts. Secondary: genus/numerus/status plus other morphology exact-feature candidate recall **conditional on a published nonempty target feature** and morph coverage; explicit denominator for each. Report no-scoreable-label counts without silently excluding them.
- Test exact source group exclusions, partial feature fields, role-not-inferred boundary, target POS/lemma/cotext/German reference mutation invariance, independent context-support thresholds, duplicate text vote collapsing, safe source hash tamper refusals, and complete test denominator. No model selection on the new temple results.
- No paid inference, no neural training, no independent semantic adjudication, and no points until separately accepted scientifically. If source rights/bytes/census checks fail, report blocked; do not substitute already exposed corpus.
