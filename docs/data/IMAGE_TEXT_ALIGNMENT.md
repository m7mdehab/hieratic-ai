# Image-to-text alignment

`schemas/alignment_manifest.schema.json` defines alignments between acquisition object identity, DATA-004 page/region IDs, and line/sign/token targets. The CLI validates both the DATA-002 acquisition schema and the DATA-004 annotation schema/invariants before checking cross-file identity, region/page membership, and page/line reading order. Empty target sets require an explicit omission/gap/damage/unresolved relation; unmatched text and image regions are never silently discarded. Coordinates remain owned by DATA-004 annotation geometry and its declared coordinate system; alignment stores stable region IDs rather than copying or transforming coordinates.

Relations include one-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-one, ligature, omission, gap, damage, editorial restoration and unresolved. Competing hypotheses, confidence, reviewer state and provenance are retained. Dependencies are cycle-checked, and contradictory resolved mappings for one image region fail. Gold eligibility is derived, never trusted from user input: only resolved, reviewed/adjudicated, reviewer-attributed, confidence-bearing alignments with explicit targets, provenance and no competing hypotheses can be eligible; damage, omissions, gaps, restorations, unresolved/disputed targets and benchmark material remain excluded.

Run `python -m tools.alignment validate MANIFEST.yaml --acquisition ACQUISITION.yaml --annotation ANNOTATION.yaml [--output FILE]`. The checked-in example uses the DATA-002 and DATA-004 contracts with synthetic identifiers and includes no image or transcription answer. Synthetic fixtures are exempt only from real acquisition-completion requirements; they do not imply that conditional rights are cleared. Real assets still require DATA-002's independent acquisition admission checks. This task has no dependency on DATA-003 preprocessing.

## W9 reference-only RIME geometry

`data/alignment/w9_rime/CAT1883-CAT2095-recto-reference-geometry.json` is intentionally a separate pointer packet, not an ordinary DATA-006 alignment manifest. Run `python -m tools.alignment validate-reference [PACKET.json]`. It pins the article-hosted RIME Fig. 6 TIFF and carries three manually estimated column-envelope boxes, edition section pointers, and published line counts. The article's text has no verified reuse license in this project, so the packet contains no transliteration, translation, line text, or DATA-004 identifiers. The article itself reports an ambiguous assignment around a line-1 boundary; the packet records that ambiguity and does not resolve it.

Coordinates are approximate column envelopes derived from the article's published 410 mm support width and column widths/gaps plus visual inspection of Fig. 6. They are unreviewed and do not localize individual line baselines; `line_level_alignment=false`, `gold_scoreable=false`, and no source registry or training admission is inferred. This supports image/citation review only. It does **not** satisfy the DATA-006 requirement for a reviewed, exact image-to-line alignment or any DATA-008 corpus admission criterion.

## W10 lawful source-exact line-pair investigation

Run `python -m tools.alignment validate-w10-pair [PACKET.json]`. The default packet is `data/alignment/w10_lawful_line_pair/CAT1883-CAT2095-verso-pleyte-line-2.json`, validated against its adjacent versioned schema. This is a fail-closed, fixed-source research packet: it pins RIME Fig. 8's TIFF bytes, view, physical support, image-use evidence, edition witness, page/item/plate pointers, public-domain evidence and coordinate asset hash. It contains no edition transcription or image bytes. A candidate line envelope is recorded in original-source pixels; the validator refuses mismatched source hashes, derivative coordinates, side/support identity, absent rights evidence, unsupported edition locators, invalid geometry, or any attempt to mark the item reviewed or gold.

**This is not a verified image-to-line match.** The RIME 2022 article identifies Cat.1883 + Cat.2095 as one papyrus of five joined fragments, labels Fig. 8 as the verso, and describes nine lines on that face. The image-use policy allows RIME figure images under CC BY 2.0; it does not grant a license to reuse the article's transcription. For the edition pointer, RIME cites W. Pleyte and F. Rossi, *Papyrus de Turin*, vol. 1, printed p. 41, and vol. 2, Plate XXIX. The scanned 1869-era edition is marked public domain by Commons. Only the bibliographic locus is represented here. It identifies printed p. 41, numbered item 2, but the plate was not visually cross-checked in this investigation and no independent palaeographic reviewer confirmed that the approximate Fig. 8 envelope is that exact line. The bounds `[2800, 1690, 6500, 2020]` are approximate candidate geometry, not reviewed baseline/line segmentation. The packet must remain `proposed_unreviewed_candidate`, with zero DATA-004 links, no gold, and training blocked.

## W14 physical image/edition comparison: Plate XXIX

The executable dossier at `data/alignment/w14_physical_line_evidence/plate_xxix_correspondence.json` records a visual comparison of the original RIME Fig. 8 TIFF with the source scans of Pleyte/Rossi vol. 1 printed p. 41 and vol. 2 Plate XXIX. Validate it with `python -m tools.alignment validate-w14-dossier`. The adjacent JSON Schema fixes source identities, hashes, inspected page indices, rendering parameters, transformations, rights evidence, and the negative review state. To independently verify locally obtained original files, run `python -m tools.alignment verify-w14-assets --rime-image FIG8.tif --volume-1 VOLUME1.pdf --volume-2 VOLUME2.pdf`; this read-only command checks each source's SHA-256 and byte count and checks the TIFF's pixel dimensions using the Python standard library.

The result resolves the W10 p. 41 item 2 candidate negatively. The pinned RIME figure is the **verso** of one joined five-fragment support. On the visually inspected Plate XXIX, the right-hand panel is captioned as the Ramses II text and contains nine numbered rows; the p. 41 item 2 entry is a text entry associated with that **recto** panel. The two pointers therefore fail the side-identity check and do not identify the same written line. The W10 box `[2800,1690,6500,2020]` remains an approximate envelope on the original Fig. 8 TIFF; it cannot be transformed into the recto line locus.

Plate XXIX also contains a separate upper-left reverse panel. That panel is sparse and numbered one through seven. It establishes that the historical plate depicts reverse-side material, but this comparison did not establish which specific visible line corresponds to which line in the photograph. Shared support identity, writing direction, or similar row count is insufficient for line identification. Its line-level correspondence remains unresolved. The earlier W10 packet is retained unchanged as the historical, unreviewed candidate record; this W14 dossier adds the source-backed side-mismatch finding rather than rewriting that earlier record.

The reproduction record gives exact SHA-256 and byte counts for both source PDFs, the RIME TIFF, and the two inspected PDF page renders. The historical volumes are recorded separately from the modern RIME article. Only RIME image reuse is claimed under the cited CC BY 2.0 image policy; that image policy does not grant rights to the article's scholarly transcription. The historical scanned volumes carry separate PDM 1.0 evidence. No transcription or raw image/PDF bytes are committed. Rendered-page panel envelopes are approximate inspection coordinates only; no cross-asset geometric transform was asserted.

W14 inspected **one independent physical support** (Cat.1883 + Cat.2095 jointly), established **zero exact or independently expert-reviewed line pairs**, and authorizes **zero gold labels and zero training admission**. Benchmark overlap remains unresolved and quarantined. The dossier validator rejects changes to support, side, page/item/plate, figure, source hashes, rendered derivatives, rights references, transformation history, benchmark state, candidate geometry, reviewer identity, or gold/admission flags. Synthetic tests for this research contract use no ancient textual content.

The fallback review uses the source-backed candidate matrix above. Cat.2044/013's image is identified as CC0, but the associated partner PDF is CC BY-NC and does not establish a permitted line-text reuse route. The Met Heqanakht object has a public-domain image but no line-addressable edition with verified compatible text rights in this evidence set. The British Museum alternative carries CC BY-NC-SA image terms, and no matching text license was established. These routes therefore do not supply a lawful image-plus-edition line pair under this task's constraints; they remain excluded or metadata-only.
