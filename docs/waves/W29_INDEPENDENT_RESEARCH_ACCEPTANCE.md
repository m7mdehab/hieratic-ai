- Genuine original museum JPEG decoding → resized grayscale 1536×768 (this distorts original aspect ratio; **not yet OCR-ready geometry**), 36k train pixels ×3 Gaussian synthetic noises =108k paired training pixels, train-only 20% dev chose ridge alpha=1.0. Three fixed noise sigmas (8/16/24) independently evaluated on **388,362** original-source heldout pixels per sigma from the **other Met accession**, with no test-side tuning.
- At noise σ16, **noisy PSNR 24.04980 dB**, trained 3×3 ridge **30.19756 dB**, fixed untrained mean filter **32.01977 dB**; MAE 0.05005266, 0.02453351 and 0.01893991 respectively. At σ8 ridge 34.88708 vs mean 35.21667; at σ24 ridge 27.07996 vs mean 29.40817. **Learned model helps versus noisy input but loses to simple filter at EVERY sigma**; therefore no superiority claim.
- Stronger than historical published facsimiles: genuine photographed manuscript pixels from two distinct museum accessions; **not** independently verified disjoint scribes or sealed benchmark lineage, no ink-label segmentation gold, no Hieratic sign recognition or diplomatic reading. No source image/derived patches copied into Git.
