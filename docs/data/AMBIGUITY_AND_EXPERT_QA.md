# Ambiguity and expert QA

`schemas/annotation_review.schema.json` defines independent reviewer assignments, conflict-of-interest flags and groups, per-reviewer decisions, adjudication records, and explicit case-state history. Decisions are append-only: a correction must retain the earlier decision and point to it with `supersedes_decision_id`; replacement/deletion fails validation. The tool enforces the deterministic transition graph and requires independent, non-conflicted reviewers. When blinding is enabled, neither model predictions nor peer decisions may be visible in the packet.

Outcomes preserve certain readings, acceptable alternatives, uncertainty, illegibility/damage, missing gold and editorial restoration. Disagreement remains unresolved until an independent adjudicator records an evidence-linked outcome. No synthetic case is a claim of expert work.

`python -m tools.annotation_review validate PACKET.yaml --annotation ANNOTATION.yaml` validates DATA-004 references, assignments, decision evidence, state transitions and append-only history. `report` returns exact agreement with a pairwise valid denominator, disagreements, exclusions by reason, and a deterministic digest of the review/audit payload. Cases with illegible, missing or adjudication-pending gold; damage, missing-gold or restoration flags; incomplete pairs; or unscorable decisions are excluded from the agreement denominator. The denominator is independent reviewer pairs, not individual review events.

For EVAL-001, `annotation_id`, `target_type`/`target_id`, `annotation_layer`, `annotation_gold_status`, acceptable reading IDs, confidence and adjudication outcome map to its expert-review packet, gold-status, and layer-specific targets; uncertainty stays explicit and scoring still follows the metric's own eligible-unit denominator. For EVAL-005, `target_id`, evidence references, adjudication state and issue flags can be cited while an independent error-review record chooses the frozen codebook's `error_codes`, `primary_error`, `attribution_status` and contamination/exposure fields. DATA-007 does not invent EVAL-005 error labels because it has no model-output failure to classify. Review events are not performance denominators. This task does not edit either evaluation contract and does not turn adjudication agreement into model performance.
