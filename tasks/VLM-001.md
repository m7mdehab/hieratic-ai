## 1. Mission and Scientific Objectives

Establish an immutable, reproducible evaluation harness and baseline contract for Vision-Language Models (VLMs) across the four Hieratic reading stages (`identify`, `signs`, `transliterate`, `translate`).

This task operates independently from the overseer's `EVAL-003` frontier proprietary model comparison (`eval/baselines/**`). It establishes the preflight contracts, schema definitions, dual-channel scoring engine, and quarantined demonstration protocols necessary to evaluate open-weight multimodal architectures without test contamination.

## 2. Core Scientific Requirements and Boundaries

1. **Immutable Evaluation Conditions:**
   - Pre-register exact model identifiers, revisions, greedy decoding parameters (`temperature=0.0`, seed 42), and image preprocessing constraints ($\le 1024$ px longest edge, RGB).
   - Freeze zero-shot and few-shot prompt templates via cryptographic SHA-256 hashes.

2. **Quarantined Demonstration Governance & Fail-Closed Clearance:**
   - The current demonstration bank (`eval/vlm/demonstrations.yaml`) is strictly designated as `synthetic_fixture_only` with `rights_review_status: synthetic_placeholder_unreviewed`.
   - **Real few-shot inference is unconditionally blocked** on open-weight backbones (`LiveFewShotBlockedError`) until verified multi-image vision templates, exemplar pixels, and provenance receipts exist.
   - Forged rights clearance without on-disk image pixels fails closed (`UnverifiedDemonstrationError`).
   - Self-declared clearance booleans are rejected; genuine image evidence and independent checks are mandatory.

3. **Inference Capability & Hardware Barrier Disclosures:**
   - `OpenWeightVLMAdapter` includes a real, reproducible image-conditioned PyTorch/Transformers execution path.
   - In environments lacking dedicated NVIDIA CUDA GPUs ($\ge 16$ GB VRAM) or downloaded model weights, the adapter accurately identifies and reports the hardware/weights barrier and fails closed (`status: failed`, diagnostic error message), without fabricating synthetic outputs or initiating unapproved network API calls.

4. **Dual-Channel Metric Scoring & Contract Conformance:**
   - **Channel 1 (Official HieraticBench):** The pinned upstream TypeScript scorer (`bench/src/score.ts`) is authoritative. In this offline preflight harness, official scoring reports `official_scoring_status: "NOT_INTEGRATED"` and `official_hieraticbench: null`. Caller-injected arbitrary summaries are rejected.
   - **Channel 2 (Project-Native EVAL-001 Diagnostics):** In-house metrics conform strictly to `eval/metric_contract.yaml`:
     - `identify`: `SCRIPT_ACC` (direction: higher, unit: proportion)
     - `signs`: `SIGN_TOP1` (direction: higher, unit: proportion)
     - `transliterate`: `TR_CER` (direction: lower, unit: edit_error_rate, Levenshtein CER, 1.0 penalty for non-success)
     - `translate`: `TRANS_CHRF` (direction: higher, unit: score)
   - Secondary diagnostics include `TR_TER`, `DIAG_ACCURACY`, and `VLM_DIAG_BLEU_4`.

5. **Independent Universe Denominators & Admission Governance (EVAL-006):**
   - **Independently Pinned Universe:** Canonical universe (`eval/vlm/universe.yaml`) with deterministic canonical SHA-256 (`fd72f77473b0c07074a18ceafbddc40449f4f9690be99e522100b313e3b0780d`). Manifest auditing compares recorded attempts against this independent contract, catching item-dropping and denominator tampering.
   - **Dataset Admission Tiers:** Explicit tiers (`synthetic_preflight_universe`, `unverified_external_inputs`, `approved_evaluation_cohort`). External items without an audited admission receipt default to `unverified_external_inputs` and fail closed against certification.
   - **Fail-Closed Image Decoding & Integrity:** Validates on-disk image existence, PNG/JPEG magic byte verification, and SHA-256 byte hashes.
   - **Document-Clustered Bootstrap:** Confidence intervals use at least **2,000 document-clustered bootstrap resamples** ($B=2000$) clustered by `document_id`. Single or zero document clusters report a null confidence interval (`insufficient_document_clusters`).
   - **Paired Comparisons:** Strictly paired across composite identities `(item_id, rung, shot_mode, sample_index)` with mandatory condition filters (`--shot-mode-a`, `--shot-mode-b`).

6. **Promotion Prevention & Certification Gate:**
   - Manifests and score reports generated by `mock-vision-v1` are immutably tagged with `execution_tier: "synthetic_ci_fixture"` and `scientific_validity: "non_scientific_test_fixture"`.
   - `--require-certified` enforces strict fail-closed promotion checks: rejects preflight harnesses, barrier-blocked manifests, unverified external items, and forged synthetic fixtures.
