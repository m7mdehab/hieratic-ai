## Breakthrough: two independently rights-evidenced sources for the SAME physical papyrus

The [Museo Egizio Cat.1880 museum catalogue](https://collezioni.museoegizio.it/en-GB/material/Cat_1880) confirms *the Turin Strike Papyrus* is a real Hieratic administrative manuscript. Its own bibliography ties this exact accession to Willem Pleyte and Francesco Rossi’s `Papyrus de Turin`, **vol. 1 pp. 50–65 and vol. 2 plates XXXV–XLVIII**. The [original nineteenth-century text volume](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Papyrus_de_Turin._(IA_papyrusdeturin01muse).pdf) and [historical plate volume](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Papyrus_de_Turin._(IA_papyrusdeturin02muse).pdf) both have separate Commons **Public Domain Mark 1.0** statements. Modern [TPOP document 131](https://collezionepapiri.museoegizio.it/en-GB/document/131) contains **12 visible text-selector entries**, which are NOT physical written-line numbers and do NOT license editor-authored strings for ML.

The [newer original CC0 photograph p01](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_so-called_%27Strike_Papyrus%27_written_by_Amunnakht_-_Museo_Egizio_Turin_C_1880_p01.jpg) is 17,704×7,983, [newer p02](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_so-called_%27Strike_Papyrus%27_written_by_Amunnakht_-_Museo_Egizio_Turin_C_1880_p02.jpg) 17,610×7,995. Two [older CC0 p01](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_so-called_%27Strike_Papyrus%27_written_by_Amunnakht,_papyurs_-_Museo_Egizio_(Turin)_C_1880_p01.jpg) and [older CC0 p02](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_so-called_%27Strike_Papyrus%27_written_by_Amunnakht,_papyurs_-_Museo_Egizio_(Turin)_C_1880_p02.jpg) have different publication/version histories. The [same accession Commons media gallery](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:The_so-called_%27Strike_Papyrus%27_written_by_Amunnakht_-_Museo_Egizio_Turin_C_1880) has **seven image files**, including 2 TIFF and 5 JPEG, all **one original papyrus**, not seven independent support groups. Filename p01 or p02 alone does NOT prove recto/verso, page-to-plate, or image transformation.

**Scientific status:** historical facsimile-to-photograph document attribution is **source-attested**, but **NO photograph–plate pixel registration, matched individual line/stroke sequence, cross-asset ROI, independent second-reader gold or exact reuse-authorized diplomatic transcript** is established. No modern scholarly TPOP text licensing inferred from CC0 photography or nineteenth-century PDM plates.

## Two physically separate authentic museum object leads

**[Suppl.6759](https://collezioni.museoegizio.it/en-GB/material/S_6759)** is a genuine **Hieratic oracular text** on a clay ostracon; [CC0 original S.6759 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hieratic_ostracon_with_an_oracular_text,_clay_-_Museo_Egizio_(Turin)_S_6759_p01.jpg) is 3,760×3,259. Museum cites Černý notebook 18 p52 and **López 1980 p48 plates 83/83a**. None of those **modern text/edition** rights is verified as public-domain/CC0. English question prose is not Egyptian diplomatic line gold. Pair is **not** certified.

**[Cat.2169](https://collezioni.museoegizio.it/en-GB/material/Cat_2169)** is a limestone ostracon bearing **craftsmen’s identity marks/monograms**; the museum explains some come from hieroglyphs and some are arbitrary geometric marks. Exact [CC0 p01](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hieratic_ostracon_with_an_inventory,_limestone_-_Museo_Egizio_(Turin)_C_2169_p01.jpg) and [CC0 p02](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hieratic_ostracon_with_an_inventory,_limestone_-_Museo_Egizio_(Turin)_C_2169_p02.jpg) each 8,256×5,504. All [seven Commons gallery variants](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Hieratic_ostracon_with_an_inventory,_limestone_-_Museo_Egizio,_Turin_C_2169) are one object. **Contextual ambiguous hard control, NOT a guaranteed nonwriting negative and NOT continuous Egyptian text-line transliteration gold.** López 1978 source remains rights unverified.
